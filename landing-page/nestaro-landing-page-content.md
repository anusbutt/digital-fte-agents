## 2. The Problem

**Headline:** Running a small business means juggling too many channels

**Body:** Customer emails pile up. WhatsApp messages sit unanswered for hours.
Comments and DMs on Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter/X get missed. Invoices and
bookkeeping fall behind while you're busy actually running the business.
You can't be everywhere at once — and hiring someone to watch it all is
expensive and slow to train.

---

## 3. How It Works

**Headline:** Nestaro handles the busywork. You stay in control.

1. **Watch** — Nestaro monitors your Gmail, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram,
   Twitter/X, and accounting system around the clock.
2. **Draft** — When something needs a reply, a post, or an accounting action
   (like an invoice), Nestaro prepares it for you.
3. **Review** — Every draft lands in one simple approval queue. You approve,
   edit, or reject — nothing happens automatically.
4. **Done** — Approved items go out instantly, and everything is logged so you
   always have a record.

---

## 4. What Nestaro Can Do (Feature Grid)

- **Inbox management** — reads and drafts replies to business emails
- **WhatsApp replies** — drafts responses to customer messages
- **Social media** — monitors Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter/X for DMs and
  mentions, and drafts replies or posts
- **Accounting automation** — creates invoices, records payments, and tracks
  your finances
- **Weekly business briefing** — a simple summary of what came in, what Nestaro
  handled, and how your finances look
- **Full activity log** — a complete, searchable record of everything Nestaro
  has done

---

## 5. Trust & Safety

**Headline:** Nothing happens without you.

**Body:** Nestaro never sends an email, posts on social media, or processes a
payment on its own. Every single action — no matter how small — sits in your
approval queue until you say go. Think of Nestaro as a very fast, very
attentive assistant who drafts everything and waits for your signature.

---

## 6. Getting Started

**Headline:** Up and running in a day

1. **Connect your accounts** — Gmail, WhatsApp, your social profiles, and your
   accounting system.
2. **Nestaro starts watching** — it begins monitoring and preparing drafts
   right away.
3. **Check your queue** — review what Nestaro has prepared each day; approve,
   tweak, or skip.
4. **Get your weekly briefing** — a short summary of activity and finances,
   delivered automatically.
